01 // THE GAME

The game

A long way
from Earth.

Victoria “Vicky” Azinova is an engineer aboard a transport ship months out from port — recycled air, narrow decks, and cabins full of passengers and crew with reasons to keep their doors shut.

Most of her night is spent talking. Flirt, bargain, lie, or push back, then take the ship apart yourself when talking stops working.

01Romance & intimacy

Pursue the crew and passengers who interest Vicky. Explicit scenes are written into the story, not parked in a gallery.

02Dialogue that commits

Choices decide who trusts her, who wants her, and how much she is told.

03A ship to work

Search the decks from an isometric view, run quests, operate machinery, and manage what Vicky wears and carries.

04Real-time combat

Draw a weapon, watch its heat, and survive the people who would rather you disappeared.
